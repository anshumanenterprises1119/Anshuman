Field Observations

Why Product Quality Matters

Based on years of hands-on contracting experience, we have observed how cheap electrical materials always lead to major maintenance headaches down the road.

🔥

Fire & Overheating Risks

Overheating wires and loose modular switches are among the most common issues we see in low-quality installations. Substandard copper or insulation melts easily under heavy loads, creating severe fire hazards.

⚡

Voltage & Fluctuation Damage

Cheap MCBs and poorly wound cables fail to trip during short circuits or power surges. This ruins expensive commercial machinery, office servers, and home appliances in an instant.

💸

Hidden Maintenance Costs

Many builders initially choose cheaper wiring materials to reduce project cost, but after 1–2 years, maintenance issues become much more expensive. Ripping out walls to replace bad conduits is incredibly costly.

A Practical Field Observation: “In commercial projects, consistent product quality matters more than temporary price differences. When you wire a building, you're investing in 20+ years of safety, not just saving a few thousand rupees on day one.”
