Founder's Journey

A Business Forged in Field Operations

True expertise isn't learned in a classroom; it is built on active project sites. Anshuman Enterprises wasn't conceived on paper — it emerged directly from hands-on industry experience.

Before launching our supply division, Aditya Tiwari worked alongside developers, electricians, and installation crews. He actively managed detailed wiring installations, commercial setups, architectural lighting designs, and residential power networks.

"Successful projects require more than raw components. They demand technical clarity, fair value, and enduring build quality."

This deep practical exposure highlighted the hidden inefficiencies of the electrical market — specifically how commission-heavy structures and inflated pricing networks worked against the client's interests.

Time and again, Aditya saw clients forced into a compromise: either purchasing premium-tier brands at exorbitant markups, or buying substandard local items prone to failure and safety hazards.

Recognizing the need for a better choice, he established Anshuman Enterprises — a trusted sourcing partner dedicated to providing premium electrical and construction hardware goods at honest, factory-direct rates.

Industry Gaps

Solving the Core Sourcing Dilemma

We identified the key struggles facing builders and contractors in the electrical marketplace and built a service to solve them.

❌

Inflated Brand Premiums

Massive marketing campaigns and elaborate distribution networks drive up prices. Customers end up paying for logos rather than actual material value.

❌

Substandard Quality Risks

Cheaper alternatives lead to persistent maintenance problems, thermal overloading, and safety threats. Margin-driven retail stores often push these without warning.

❌

Complex Supply Chains

Multiple layers of agents, distributors, and sub-dealers mark up prices at every stage. By delivery time, costs have escalated needlessly.

✅

The Anshuman Solution

Direct manufacturer relations, verified product lifespans, flat wholesale rates, and expert-led recommendations aligned with project parameters.
