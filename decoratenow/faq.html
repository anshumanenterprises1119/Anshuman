Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DecorateNow products, LED bulb inclusions, warranties, shipping, & custom consultations.

lightbulb Product & Bulb Specifications

Are LED bulbs included with wall lights & chandeliers? expand_more
Please check the specific product title or specification table. Many of our premium integrated wall lights include warm 3000K LED modules built-in. For standard fixtures requiring separate bulbs (E27, E14, or G9 holders), recommended warm amber filament bulbs can be added to your order directly.
What light color temperature (Kelvin rating) do DecorateNow lights use? expand_more
Most of our decorative collections use 3000K (Warm Amber / Soft Warm White) to create an inviting, cozy atmosphere for living rooms, bedrooms, and dining spaces. 4000K (Natural Cool) and 3-CCT dimmable options are also available for select LED models.
Are DecorateNow lights compatible with dimmers? expand_more
Yes! All dimmable-rated fixtures and filament bulbs support standard trailing-edge phase dimmers as well as smart home automation systems (like Philips Hue, Alexa, or Google Home smart switches).

local_shipping Delivery & Glass Safety

How do you package delicate glass chandeliers & wall sconces? expand_more
All fragile items are shipped in multi-layer padded foam molds and heavy-duty corrugated crates. Every order includes 100% transit insurance — in the rare event of damage, we dispatch a free replacement component immediately.
What is the estimated delivery time for Greater Noida & NCR orders? expand_more
Orders within Greater Noida, Noida, and Delhi-NCR are delivered within 24 to 48 hours via express dispatch. Pan-India deliveries take 3 to 6 business days via insured courier partners.

verified Warranty & Installation

What warranty is provided on DecorateNow products? expand_more
All DecorateNow light fixtures come with a 1-Year Comprehensive Warranty backed by Anshuman Enterprises covering drivers, LED chips, and structural defects.
Do you provide installation services in Greater Noida? expand_more
Yes! On-site installation and ceiling mounting can be arranged through our sister arm, Aditya Contractor, by certified electrician teams in Greater Noida & NCR.
