✨ About DecorateNow

Illuminating Extraordinary Spaces

DecorateNow is the luxury lighting & interior decor brand by Anshuman Enterprises — dedicated to crafting warm, ambient, and architectural light fixtures for homes, luxury villas, and boutique spaces.

Our Vision & Heritage

Where Engineering Meets Pure Artistry

Founded in Greater Noida as an extension of Anshuman Enterprises' 15+ years of electrical engineering excellence, DecorateNow was born out of a simple belief: Lighting shouldn't just illuminate a room — it should transform its soul.

We bridge the gap between high-end architectural lighting showrooms and direct-to-customer affordability. From hand-blown amber glass pendants to modern minimalist chandeliers and warm CRI 90+ LED strips, every DecorateNow product is engineered for visual elegance and long-lasting durability.
