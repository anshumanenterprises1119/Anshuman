DecorateNow Privacy Policy

At DecorateNow, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy details how we handle data collected when you browse our boutique catalog, place orders, or request interior lighting consultations.

1. Information We Collect

When you purchase items or request order updates from DecorateNow, we collect minimal required details including your full name, shipping address, contact phone number, and email address.
