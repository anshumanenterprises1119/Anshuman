Reliable Power Distribution Products in Greater Noida

The Distribution Board (DB) is the heart of every building's electrical system. It controls, distributes, and protects every circuit in your home or office. Using substandard MCBs or poorly designed DB boxes is not just risky—it's dangerous. Anshuman Enterprises supplies only ISI-marked, BIS-certified protection devices from the world's most trusted manufacturers.

Our technical team can help you design an optimal DB layout based on your building's load profile, ensuring each circuit is properly sized and protected against overloads, short circuits, and earth faults.

Our Product Range

  • MCB (Miniature Circuit Breakers): Available in B, C, and D curves from 0.5A to 63A for precise overcurrent protection.
  • RCCB (Residual Current Circuit Breakers): Essential life-saving devices that detect earth leakage and cut power in milliseconds to prevent electrocution.
  • Isolators: 40A to 100A main switches for complete power isolation during maintenance.
  • RCBO (Combined MCB+RCCB): Space-saving single-module devices providing both overcurrent and leakage protection.
  • Distribution Boards (SPN/TPN/DBs): Metal and plastic enclosures from 4-way to 24-way for residential and commercial installations.
  • Changeover Switches: Manual and automatic transfer switches for DG-to-mains changeover in societies and offices.

Understanding Your Protection Needs

Many homeowners don't know the difference between an MCB and an RCCB. An MCB protects your wiring from overloads, while an RCCB protects people from electric shock. Modern Indian electrical codes (IS 732) recommend having both in every home. Our team provides free consultations to ensure your DB is compliant and your family is safe.

For commercial and industrial setups, we also supply complete LT Panel components including bus bars, CT/PT units, and energy meters to ensure your power distribution is efficient and audit-ready.
